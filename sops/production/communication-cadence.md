# Communication Cadence

**Owner:** CEM (Customer Experience Manager)

**Applies to:** Every project, from signing through 30-day follow-up.

**Related:** `customer-journey.md`

## Purpose

One-page quick reference for every customer touchpoint across the project
lifecycle. The Customer Journey is the playbook; this is the CEM's daily
cheat sheet.

## Cadence At A Glance

| When | Channel | Who | SOP |
|---|---|---|---|
| Within minutes of signing | Automated text + email | CRM (Go High Level) | `customer-journey.md` Phase 1 Step 1 |
| Same day as signing | Email | CEM | `project-email-exterior.md` / `-interior.md` / `-stain.md` |
| Within 24 hours of signing | Phone | CEM | `cem-intro-call.md` |
| Weekly until start date | Text | CEM | `customer-journey.md` Phase 2 |
| ~1 week before start (Friday) | Text | CEM | `customer-journey.md` Phase 2 |
| Day 1 on site | In-person | CEM + crew leader | `pre-job-walkthrough.md` |
| Every day crew is on site | In-person | CEM | `daily-site-visit.md` |
| Every evening crew is on site | Text | CEM | `daily-customer-update.md` |
| Final day on site | In-person | Crew leader | `final-inspection-form.md` |
| Final day on site | In-person | Crew leader | `after-project-survey.md` |
| Final day on site | In-person | CEM | `cem-closeout.md`, `review-request.md` |
| Within 48 hours of completion | Handwritten note | CEM | `thank-you-note.md` |
| ~30 days after completion | Phone | CEM | `thirty-day-follow-up.md` |
| Ongoing | Newsletter / reactivations | Marketing | `marketing-handoff.md` |

## Principles

- **Consistency beats perfection.** Pick a weekly update day, own it.
- **Silence is the enemy.** If you don't know yet, say so — don't go dark.
- **Under-promise on timing.** Two-week window at sign; narrow it weekly;
  lock it on Friday.
- **Open question on every daily update.** Always invite feedback; surface
  issues before they become complaints.
