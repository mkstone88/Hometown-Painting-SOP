# CEM Closeout — Emotional Close

**Owner:** CEM (Customer Experience Manager)

**Applies to:** Every project, immediately after the Final Inspection is signed.

**Related:** `final-inspection-form.md`, `after-project-survey.md`, `review-request.md`

## Purpose

Turn "client" into "raving fan." After the crew leader has signed the
customer off on the Final Inspection, the CEM's role is not
administrative — it is to create an emotional high point that generates
the five-star review, the referral card passed to a neighbor, and the
story the customer tells at dinner.

## Scope

Happens on the final day of work, after `final-inspection-form.md` is
complete. On smaller or simpler jobs, the CEM may complete this portion
by phone immediately after the crew leader's walkthrough rather than in
person. Default is in person.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the Final Inspection is complete and signed.
2. Congratulate the customer genuinely on the finished project.
   Reference a specific detail you noticed.
3. Deliver the warranty certificate. *(Pending — see system gap below.)*
4. Present the closing gift. *(Pending — see system gap below.)*
5. Make the review request using the QR code (see `review-request.md`).
6. Confirm the After Project Survey was completed; if not, capture it
   before you leave.
7. Note any referral conversations, reference willingness, or
   outstanding commitments in the project record.

## System Gaps (Deferred)

- **Warranty certificate template.** Target: branded, printable or
  emailable certificate with job address, date, warranty terms, and
  Hometown contact info. Until built, acknowledge warranty verbally and
  tell the customer a certificate will follow by email.
- **Closing gift.** Target: custom laser-engraved woodworking item with
  Hometown logo, ~$20–$30 per unit from a local woodworking contact.
  Until built, skip this step and document it in the project record.
