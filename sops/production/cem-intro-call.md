# CEM Intro Call

**Owner:** CEM (Customer Experience Manager)
**Applies to:** Every new signed project, within 24 hours of contract signing.
**Related:** `customer-journey.md` (Phase 1, Step 3)

## Purpose

The first direct touchpoint between the CEM and the customer. Performs the
relationship transfer from salesperson to project owner. Reassures the
customer they made the right call and signals that a professional team has
taken the wheel.

## Scope

Keep the call under 5 minutes. It is not a planning call — the project
email (already sent) collects the logistics. This call is purely about
introduction, reassurance, and expectation-setting.

## Procedure

1. Review the signed agreement and project email so you can reference
   specifics.
2. Call the customer within 24 hours of the contract being signed.
3. Cover the four points below.
4. If they have questions you can't answer on the spot, note them and
   follow up in writing by end of day.
5. Log the call in the CRM.

## The Four Points

- **Introduction.** Who the CEM is and their role.
- **Reassurance.** Confirm the customer made the right call and the
  project is in good hands.
- **Timeline.** Brief overview of what to expect and when.
- **Next steps.** Remind them to complete the project email if they
  haven't already.

## Script

> Hi [Name], this is [CEM Name] from Hometown Painting — I'm the Customer
> Experience Manager here and I'll be your main point of contact from here
> on out. Matt handles the sales side and I take over once the project is
> underway to make sure everything goes smoothly for you. I just wanted to
> introduce myself and let you know we're really looking forward to your
> project. You should have received a project email from us — once we get
> those details back from you we can get you locked into the schedule. Do
> you have any questions for me at this point?
