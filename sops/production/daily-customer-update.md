# Daily Customer Update

**Owner:** CEM (Customer Experience Manager)
**Applies to:** Every evening that a crew is on site.
**Related:** `daily-site-visit.md`, `customer-journey.md` (Phase 4)

## Purpose

Close every working day with a short, warm text that reports progress,
previews tomorrow, and invites feedback. The open-ended question is the
most important part — it gives the customer an easy opening to surface
concerns before they become complaints.

## Procedure

1. Before leaving the site, note what was accomplished today and what the
   crew plans to do tomorrow.
2. Send the text from the CEM's phone later that day — not from a
   shared/automated number.
3. Keep it short. Three sentences.
4. If the customer replies with a concern, acknowledge within the hour
   and address the same day (see `handling-onsite-issues.md`).

## Text Template

> Hi [Name]! Quick update from your project — the crew finished [what was
> done today] today and is planning to tackle [what's next] tomorrow.
> Everything looking good from your end?

## Principles

- **Specific, not generic.** "Finished prep on the east side" beats
  "made progress today."
- **Always ask.** The closing question is non-negotiable. It's how small
  issues surface early.
- **Own the response.** Do not punt follow-ups to the next day — silence
  after a concern is how trust erodes.
