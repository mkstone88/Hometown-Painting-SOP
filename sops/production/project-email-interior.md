# Project Email — Interior

**Owner:** CEM (Customer Experience Manager)

**Applies to:** Every signed interior painting project, sent the same day the contract is signed.

**Related:** `customer-journey.md` (Phase 1, Step 2)

## Purpose

Gather the logistics needed to execute an interior job — colors, sheens,
furniture handling, valuables, storage, pet arrangements — in a single
well-organized email.

## Procedure

1. Wait until the signed contract hits the inbox and the CRM has fired the
   automated confirmation text/email.
2. Personalize the template below with the customer's name.
3. Send from the CEM's Hometown email address.
4. Log the send in the CRM so it's tracked against the project.
5. If the customer hasn't replied within 3 business days, follow up.

## Email Template

> **Subject:** [Client Name] — 5 Action Items for Your Painting Project! [Please Reply]

> Hi [Name],
>
> We're thrilled to have you as part of the Hometown family — your project is on the schedule and we're looking forward to making this a great experience from start to finish. Before we get rolling, we just need a few quick things from you:
>
> **#1 Colors & Sheens.** Please reply with the color name and number (example: Alabaster SW 7008) and sheen (example: eggshell) for each surface:
>
> - Interior Walls:
> - Woodwork:
>
> If you would like help selecting colors, please let us know that as well. We offer a free color consult and samples for all our clients.
>
> **#2 Valuables & Sentimental Items.** Before we arrive, please remove any irreplaceable, valuable, or sentimental items from the work area entirely — family photos, heirlooms, antiques, and anything you'd be heartbroken to see damaged. We want to make sure what matters most to you is safely out of the way.
>
> **#3 Furniture.** We'll move furniture to the center of the room and cover everything with plastic. The day before we arrive, please clear the tops of furniture and remove any items stored inside pieces in the work area. If anything is too heavy for you to move, just let us know and we'll plan around it.
>
> **#4 Tool & Supply Storage.** Please let us know where you'd like us to store our tools and paint supplies during the project.
>
> **#5 Pets.** Please plan to secure your pets away from the work area for their safety and comfort.
>
> If you have any questions in the meantime, just reply here or call us at (405) 870-2201.
>
> Thanks so much!
> Matt & the Hometown Painting Team
