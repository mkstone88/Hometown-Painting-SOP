# Marketing System Handoff

**Owner:** CEM (initiates), Marketing (takes over)
**Applies to:** Every project, immediately after the 30-day follow-up call.
**Related:** `thirty-day-follow-up.md`, `after-project-survey.md`

## Purpose

Cleanly transfer the customer relationship out of active CEM management
and into the long-term marketing system. After this handoff, the
relationship lives in marketing until the customer is ready to hire
again.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the 30-day follow-up call is complete and logged.
2. Ensure the customer is on the monthly newsletter list.
3. Ensure the customer is flagged for semi-annual reactivation campaigns.
4. If the customer indicated willingness to serve as a reference on the
   After Project Survey, add them to the references list.
5. If a referral was captured during the 30-day call, confirm sales is
   following up within 24 hours.
6. Mark the project "Closed" in the CRM.

## CEM's Job Is Done

After the handoff, the CEM has no further obligations for this customer.
If the customer reaches out directly in the future, warmly redirect or
re-engage — but proactive outreach is the marketing system's job now.
