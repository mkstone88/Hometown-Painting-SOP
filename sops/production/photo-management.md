# Photo Management (Company Cam)

**Owner:** CEM (leads), Crew Leader (contributes)
**Applies to:** Every project, from Day One Kickoff through Final Inspection.
**Status:** Draft — workflow still being defined. Update as the team uses Company Cam in practice.

## Purpose

A single, shared system for capturing and organizing every photo
associated with a project: before photos, pre-existing condition
documentation, progress shots, quality documentation, and finished
photos. Replaces the old "text to the office" placeholder referenced in
the Customer Journey.

## Current State

- Tool: **Company Cam** (adopted April 2026).
- Workflow: still being defined.
- This SOP is a placeholder. Update it as we land on conventions.

## Known Touchpoints

| Moment | Who | What to capture |
|---|---|---|
| Day One Kickoff | CEM | Before photos of every side / area in scope. |
| Day One Kickoff | Crew Leader | Pre-existing damage or conditions noted on `pre-job-walkthrough.md`. |
| Daily site visit | CEM | Branding shot (yard sign, presentable jobsite) for marketing. |
| Daily site visit | CEM | Any quality issue flagged on the `job-site-visit-checklist.md`. |
| Final Inspection | Crew Leader | Finished photos of every side / area in scope. |
