# Handwritten Thank-You Note

**Owner:** CEM (Customer Experience Manager)

**Applies to:** Every completed project, written and mailed within 48 hours of job completion.

**Related:** `customer-journey.md` (Phase 6)

## Purpose

Make the customer feel like a person thought about them — not like they
received a mass mailing. A handwritten note after the job is the small
gesture that turns a good experience into a story they tell.

## Procedure

1. Within 48 hours of job completion, write a handwritten note (pen on
   good stock — no printed cards).
2. Include two Hometown referral business cards in the envelope.
3. Keep the note to three or four sentences.
4. Reference something specific about the project or customer — the
   color, a comment they made, their pet, anything real.
5. Sign as "Matt, [CEM Name], and the Hometown team."
6. Drop in the mail the same day.
7. Log in the CRM.

## Template (Example)

> Dear [Name], thank you so much for trusting Hometown Painting with your
> home — it was truly a pleasure working with you. The [color/project
> detail] turned out beautifully and we hope you enjoy it for years to
> come. If anyone you know is thinking about a painting project, we'd
> love the chance to take care of them the same way we took care of you.
>
> With gratitude,
> Matt, [CEM Name], and the Hometown team.

## Principles

- **Personal, not templated.** The example is a starting point, not a
  copy-paste. One specific detail transforms the note.
- **Brief.** Three or four sentences is enough. Longer feels performative.
- **Mail it fast.** Inside of 48 hours, every time.
