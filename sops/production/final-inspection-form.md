# Final Inspection & Payment Authorization

**Owner:** Crew Leader (runs the inspection with the customer)
**Applies to:** Every project, on the last day of work before payment is requested.
**Related:** `customer-journey.md` (Phase 5), `after-project-survey.md`, `cem-closeout.md`

## Purpose

A joint walkthrough between the crew leader and the customer to confirm the
work is complete, fix anything on the spot, and get written sign-off that
triggers final payment. This is the moment a project goes from "in progress"
to "done."

## Procedure

1. Crew leader schedules the walkthrough with the customer at a time both
   can attend in person.
2. Walk every area of the project together with the form in hand.
3. For each item, note any corrective action needed. Fix it on the spot —
   the crew leader is present precisely because they can.
4. Both the crew leader and the customer initial every item after it is
   confirmed done or corrected.
5. Once every item is signed off, collect the customer's signature on the
   payment authorization statement.
6. Hand off to the After Project Survey (`after-project-survey.md`).
7. Notify the CEM — the project is cleared for final invoicing and for the
   CEM Closeout (`cem-closeout.md`).
