# 30-Day Follow-Up Call

**Owner:** CEM (Customer Experience Manager)

**Applies to:** Every completed project, approximately one month after job completion.

**Related:** `customer-journey.md` (Phase 6), `review-request.md`, `marketing-handoff.md`

## Purpose

A brief check-in roughly a month after completion to make sure the
customer is still happy, capture a Google review if they didn't leave one
at closeout, and ask for referrals while the experience is still fresh.

## Procedure

1. Calendar the call for ~30 days after job completion.
2. Review the project file and After Project Survey before dialing.
3. Place the call (not a text). Leave a voicemail if they don't answer
   and try again the next day.
4. Cover the three purposes below.
5. Log the call outcome in the CRM.
6. If a referral name is captured, pass it to sales for follow-up within
   24 hours.
7. Hand off to the marketing system (`marketing-handoff.md`).

## Three Purposes

- Check in on how they're enjoying the finished project.
- Request a Google review if they didn't leave one at closeout
  (`review-request.md`).
- Ask if anyone comes to mind who might need painting work done.

## Script

> Hi [Name], this is [CEM Name] from Hometown Painting — just calling to
> check in and make sure everything is still looking great about a month
> out. [Pause for response.] That's wonderful to hear. I also wanted to
> follow up — if you haven't had a chance to leave us a Google review
> yet, it would mean so much to us. And if anyone in your neighborhood
> or circle has been thinking about painting, we'd love an introduction
> — we'd take great care of them. Is there anyone who comes to mind?

## Handling Referrals

If the customer mentions a potential referral, get the name and contact
information on the spot. Pass it to the sales team the same day and
ensure sales reaches out within 24 hours.
