# Review Request

**Owner:** CEM (Customer Experience Manager)
**Applies to:** Every project, during the CEM Closeout.
**Related:** `cem-closeout.md`, `thirty-day-follow-up.md`

## Purpose

Capture the Google review at the moment of peak customer satisfaction.
Reviews from families like our customers are how future families find us,
and asking on the spot with a QR code produces dramatically higher
completion than asking later by email.

## Procedure

1. Wait until the Final Inspection is signed and the CEM Closeout is
   underway.
2. Pull out the printed QR code that links directly to the Hometown
   Google Business profile review page.
3. Deliver the script below.
4. Walk the customer through leaving the review on their phone if they're
   willing.
5. If they decline or want to do it later, note it in the project record
   and follow up during the 30-day call (`thirty-day-follow-up.md`).
6. Never leave without attempting the ask.

## Script

> We're so glad you're happy with how everything turned out — it was a
> pleasure working with you. One small favor: if you're willing, a
> Google review means the world to a small local company like ours. It's
> how families like yours find us. Here's a QR code — it takes about 60
> seconds and goes straight to our Google page. Would you be willing to
> do that for us right now?
