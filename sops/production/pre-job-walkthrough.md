# Pre-Job Walkthrough Checklist

**Owner:** CEM (leads), Crew Leader (attends)
**Applies to:** Day One Kickoff of every project, before any paint is applied.
**Related:** `customer-journey.md` (Phase 3)

## Purpose

Align the customer and crew on every detail before a single drop of paint is
applied. Prevents misunderstandings, documents pre-existing conditions, and
signals that Hometown runs a tight ship.

## Procedure

1. Meet the customer at the agreed time (typically 8:00 AM on start day).
2. Work through the checklist below in order. Every item must be covered,
   even on small jobs.
3. Have the customer initial pre-existing condition documentation and
   acknowledge anything flagged.
4. CEM takes before photos. Crew leader documents any damage noted.
5. Do not leave the site until the crew is set up and has everything they
   need (paint, tools, scope, customer preferences).

## Meeting With The Customer

- [ ] Introduce the crew and crew leader.
- [ ] Review the signed Agreement — confirm the scope for the project.
- [ ] Confirm colors, sheens, and paint choices one last time.
- [ ] Confirm working hours. Customer preferred work hours: __________
- [ ] Confirm material and equipment storage location: __________
- [ ] Confirm payment terms — final payment is due after the final
  walkthrough.
- [ ] Confirm communication preferences (text, call, or email).

## Jobsite Conditions

- [ ] Document pre-existing conditions (previous overspray, existing
  damage, anticipated problems). Have the client acknowledge in writing.
- [ ] Take before photos (CEM — upload to Company Cam).
- [ ] Place yard sign.
- [ ] Distribute door hangers to neighboring homes.

## Before Leaving

- [ ] Crew has paint and it matches the signed agreement.
- [ ] Crew has all required tools on site.
- [ ] Crew leader briefed on customer preferences and any special
  instructions.
