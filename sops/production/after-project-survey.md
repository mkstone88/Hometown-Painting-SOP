# After Project Survey (NPS)

**Owner:** Crew Leader (collects), CEM (follows up on responses)

**Applies to:** Every project, immediately after the Final Inspection is signed.

**Related:** `final-inspection-form.md`, `cem-closeout.md`, `marketing-handoff.md`

## Purpose

Capture the customer's candid feedback at the moment of peak satisfaction,
and surface any reference willingness for the marketing system.

## Procedure

1. Present the survey right after the customer signs the Final Inspection
   and Payment Authorization.
2. Stay with them while they fill it out — do not email it for later. On
   the spot has a near-100% completion rate; emailed surveys do not.
3. Capture the completed survey in the project file.
4. CEM reviews every survey response within 48 hours. Any score of 0–6
   triggers a same-day call to the customer.
5. If the customer indicated willingness to serve as a reference, flag
   them in the CRM and hand off to marketing (`marketing-handoff.md`).

## NPS Response Handling

| Score | Category | CEM Action |
|---|---|---|
| 9–10 | Promoter | Ask for Google review on the spot (see `review-request.md`). |
| 7–8 | Passive | Ask what would have made it a 10 — log the answer. |
| 0–6 | Detractor | Same-day call from CEM. Escalate to Matt if unresolved in 24 hours. |
