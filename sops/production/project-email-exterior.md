# Project Email — Exterior

**Owner:** CEM (Customer Experience Manager)

**Applies to:** Every signed exterior painting project, sent the same day the contract is signed.

**Related:** `customer-journey.md` (Phase 1, Step 2)

## Purpose

Gather the logistics needed to execute an exterior job — colors, sheens,
scheduling, storage, pet arrangements — in a single well-organized email
that signals professionalism to the customer.

## Procedure

1. Wait until the signed contract hits the inbox and the CRM has fired the
   automated confirmation text/email.
2. Personalize the template below with the customer's name.
3. Send from the CEM's Hometown email address.
4. Log the send in the CRM so it's tracked against the project.
5. If the customer hasn't replied within 3 business days, follow up.

## Email Template

> **Subject:** [Client Name] — Your Hometown Painting Project — A Few Quick Details

> Hi [Name],
>
> We're thrilled to have you as part of the Hometown family — your project is on the schedule and we're looking forward to making this a great experience from start to finish. Before we get rolling, we just need a few quick things from you:
>
> **#1 Colors & Sheens.** Please reply with the color name and number (example: Alabaster SW 7008) and sheen (example: eggshell) for each surface:
>
> - Eaves and Trim:
> - Porch Decks:
> - Accents and Trim:
>
> If you would like help selecting colors, please let us know that as well. We offer a free color consult and samples for all our clients.
>
> **#2 Scheduling.** Let us know if any start dates work particularly well for you, or if there are any days that don't work. We'll keep you updated as we nail down your exact start date.
>
> **#3 Tool & Supply Storage.** Please let us know where you'd like us to store our tools, paint, and supplies during the job.
>
> **#4 Outdoor Items.** The day before your project begins, please move any furniture off porches, decks, and from around the work area.
>
> **#5 Pets.** Please plan to board or secure your pets away from the work area for their safety and comfort.
>
> If you have any questions in the meantime, just reply here or call us at (405) 870-2201.
>
> Talk soon,
> Matt & the Hometown Painting Team

## Notes

- The email should feel warm and organized — not like a form.
- Reply to each customer's response personally; do not wait for the full set
  of answers before acknowledging.
