# Project Email — Stain

**Owner:** CEM (Customer Experience Manager)
**Applies to:** Every signed staining project, sent the same day the contract is signed.
**Related:** `customer-journey.md` (Phase 1, Step 2)

## Purpose

Gather the logistics needed to execute a staining job — stain color,
scheduling, outdoor items, neighbor access, pets — in a single
well-organized email.

## Procedure

1. Wait until the signed contract hits the inbox and the CRM has fired the
   automated confirmation text/email.
2. Personalize the template below with the customer's name.
3. Send from the CEM's Hometown email address.
4. Log the send in the CRM so it's tracked against the project.
5. If the customer hasn't replied within 3 business days, follow up.

## Email Template

> **Subject:** [Client Name] — 5 Action Items for Your Staining Project!

> Hi [Name],
>
> We're thrilled to have you as part of the Hometown family — your project is on the schedule and we're looking forward to making this a great experience from start to finish. Before we get rolling, we just need a few quick things from you:
>
> **#1 Stain Color.** Please reply with the stain color you'd like us to use (example: Wood Defender Sable Brown). If you're still deciding, let us know and we're happy to help point you in the right direction.
>
> **#2 Scheduling.** Let us know if any start dates work particularly well for you, or if there are any days that don't work. We'll keep you updated as we nail down your exact start date.
>
> **#3 Outdoor Items.** Before we arrive, please move patio furniture, landscaping, and decor away from the work area. If anything is too heavy to move, just let us know and we'll plan around it.
>
> **#4 Neighbor Access.** If any areas we need to stain require access to a neighbor's property, please give them a heads up that we'll be coming and when. A quick text from you goes a long way.
>
> **#5 Pets.** Please plan to secure your pets away from the work area for their safety and comfort.
>
> If you have any questions in the meantime, just reply here or call us at (405) 870-2201.
>
> Talk soon,
> Matt & the Hometown Painting Team
