## Purpose

Explain **when** this SOP applies and _who_ is responsible for executing it.

## Required Materials

- Drop cloths
- Painter's tape
- Primer and paint (per job spec)
- Rollers, brushes, and extension poles

## Procedure

1. Prep the room: move furniture and mask trim.
2. Apply primer to any bare or repaired surfaces.
3. Cut in corners and edges with a brush.
4. Roll the field coat from top to bottom.
5. Inspect for holidays and touch up before leaving.

## Safety Notes

Always ventilate the work area and wear appropriate PPE.
